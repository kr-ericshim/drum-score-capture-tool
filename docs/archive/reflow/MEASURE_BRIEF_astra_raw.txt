## Boundary classifier spec

A boundary record describes an **exact proposed seam and ownership plan**, not merely a barline centre.

Existing detection supplies staff coverage, stem/glyph rejection and grouped strokes; it does not certify safe separation. Reuse it as candidate generation. [system_detect.py:155–186](/Users/ericshim/Documents/myproject/score_capture_program/backend/app/pipeline/system_detect.py:155)

| Class | Required interpretation |
|---|---|
| `UNKNOWN` — default | Missing, contradictory, clipped, low-resolution or unsupported evidence; unresolved object ownership; uncertain prefix or crossing. No cut or spacing change. |
| `LOCKED` | Positive evidence that the proposed separation divides an indivisible object/span, or an explicit user prohibition. Preserve its internal geometry. |
| `SAFE` | **Every** check below passes for this exact seam; no positive veto or unresolved evidence remains. |

**SAFE requires:**

1. Five-line geometry and a genuine complete barline group, with stem/clef rejection; location agrees within `0.25g` across ≥3 usable registered source observations. Insufficient observations remain UNKNOWN in this slice.
2. Complete neighborhood, including original pixels under staff suppression; no unresolved overlay, clipping or unexplained tail content.
3. Seam outside the complete stroke/dot object, with clearance `max(1 source px, 0.15g)` from protected nonstaff ink.
4. Every nearby object has one owner; all crossing checks below complete successfully.
5. Full-height veto and pixel-accounting checks pass. A detector score or absence of connected components alone is insufficient.

Here `g` is source staff gap. Temporal agreement is a quality check, not independent semantic ground truth.

| Crossing/object | Detection on cleaned strip; consequence |
|---|---|
| Ties/slurs | Thin curved ridges, skeleton paths and conservatively linked fragments crossing the seam; inspect original and staff-suppressed views, including above/below staff. Bridge small compression gaps only in analysis. Crossing → LOCKED; incomplete arc → UNKNOWN. |
| Beams/tuplets | Thick horizontal/diagonal runs, stem-linked groups, brackets/numerals; inspect within and outside staff. Crossing group → LOCKED. |
| Lyrics/text | Group baseline-aligned components with conservative horizontal dilation, initially `0.7g`; include syllables, hyphens and extenders. A word/span crossing the seam → LOCKED. Ambiguous grouping/anchor → UNKNOWN. |
| Voltas | Long upper horizontal segment plus hook/endpoints and associated label. Lock every internal boundary of the bracket span; uncertain endpoint → UNKNOWN. |
| Repeat/double/final bars | Group thin/thick strokes and nearby paired dots, retaining tiny components. Never cut between them. Start-repeat belongs right; end-repeat left. This slice locks repeat boundaries unless direction and complete ownership are positively verified; combined/ambiguous repeats stay intact. |
| D.S./coda/segno/Fine | Protected symbol/text patches with geometric anchors; template/OCR hints may nominate, never prove absence. Preserve sign with its anchored measure; crossing → LOCKED, uncertain anchor → UNKNOWN. Do not expand navigation. |
| Multi-measure rests | Long rest body, terminals and count form one indivisible unit. Internal candidates LOCKED; `measure_count=null` unless independently verified. |
| Tempo/section labels | Preserve complete patch and anchor. Spanning tempo text locks affected measures. Section relocation is allowed only with verified ownership and the label contract below. |

Never promote UNKNOWN merely to reach four measures. Uncertain segmentation retains the complete cleaned system; supported locked spans may become contiguous groups.

## Masks

Maintain separate, versioned masks:

- **Staff-analysis mask:** five-line band and staff-relative geometry, explicitly excluding text/lyrics regions. Use for barline evidence and staff-only slack proposals.
- **Full-strip protection mask:** union of conservative ink thresholds, small-dot retention, curve/beam fragments, text groups and unresolved pixels. Text rows may **veto** safety; they never establish a barline or usable whitespace.
- **Ownership masks:** assign each lyric/text object to one measure using its anchor and extent. If it spans measures, anchor it once to a locked group without splitting it.

If notation overlaps a presumed text row, protect the union; do not erase that row. Existing envelope components are useful seeds, but their noise filtering cannot certify absence of tiny marks. [notation_envelope.py:398–452](/Users/ericshim/Documents/myproject/score_capture_program/backend/app/pipeline/notation_envelope.py:398)

## Prefix handling

- Extract prefix objects separately: clef, printed meter, opening repeat and tempo. Preserve the complete original first prefix.
- New line: reuse a **verified clef-only source patch** from compatible staff/context, with source hash, mask and placement provenance. Never synthesize or duplicate a time signature; preserve printed meter changes at their original measure.
- Clef uncertainty, contaminated patch or unresolved context prohibits the new break.
- **Initial-slice assumption:** preserve source-system starts as line starts when they contain printed prefixes; do not delete an existing clef to improve packing. Prefix-free systems may join only after endpoint checks.

The existing clef detector is a geometric hint, not verified patch extraction. [system_detect.py:189–199](/Users/ericshim/Documents/myproject/score_capture_program/backend/app/pipeline/system_detect.py:189)

## Line breaking + justification

Build ordered atomic measures/locked groups. UNKNOWN cannot become an internal split. Align staff baselines and normalize isotropically to the document gap; include prefixes, label overhang and full text envelopes in feasibility.

Use deterministic DP over legal contiguous ranges:

```text
N = natural line width, including required prefix
E = min(sum(slot expansion caps), 0.08*N)
C = min(sum(slot contraction caps), 0.03*N)

justify if W in [N-C, N+E]
otherwise allow recorded ragged line if N <= W
otherwise reject range

cost = 100*adjustment_fraction²
     + 3*(known_measure_count-4)²
     + 40*single-measure orphan
     + 25*nonfinal ragged line
```

Omit the count penalty if any count is unknown. User constraints are hard; sections do not automatically force breaks. Unreachable DP states produce a conflict, never clipping or automatic shrink. This extends the existing design’s proposed DP. [DESIGN_astra.md:321–350](/Users/ericshim/Documents/myproject/score_capture_program/docs/reflow/DESIGN_astra.md:321)

**Slots:** initially use inter-measure staff-only corridors, not arbitrary spaces between notes. Full-height protection must also clear each slot.

- Expansion/slot ≤ `min(1.2g, 0.5 × original_width)`.
- Contraction/slot ≤ `min(0.25 × original_width, original_width − 0.35g)`, floored at zero.
- Distribute adjustment by bounded water-filling; glyph/text patches translate rigidly.
- Record requested/achieved width and ragged reasons: `INSUFFICIENT_SLACK`, `LOCKED_SPAN`, `PREFIX_CONSTRAINT`, `FINAL_LINE`.
- Paginate complete lines using actual envelopes. Eight lines/page remains soft.

## Pixel accounting

Current validation counts unit IDs and placement rectangles, not pixels. [reflow_layout.py:173–200](/Users/ericshim/Documents/myproject/score_capture_program/backend/app/pipeline/reflow_layout.py:173)

For each included occurrence, identify source pixels by `(occurrence_id, asset_hash, x, y)`:

- Partition native-resolution pixels into disjoint half-open ownership masks.
- For every protected content pixel `p`, require `Σ ownership_masks[p] = 1`; destination content may not be clipped, occluded or overwritten.
- Read halos may overlap for analysis/resampling; **placement masks never overlap**. Shared barlines/dots have one owner. Padding contributes paper only.
- Split-and-reassemble at native resolution must reproduce all protected source pixel values exactly, including antialias fringes and one-pixel dots.
- Retain source IDs through layout; separately test final rendering against the approved single isotropic resampling.

**Necessary assumption:** verified staff-only scaffold is regenerable layout material. Its retained/removed/inserted pixels receive an explicit scaffold ledger; glyphs never enter it. Otherwise contraction across continuous staves must be disabled.

Original clef content is placed once; permitted reused clefs receive explicit replica IDs referencing the source. Relocated labels remain originals, not replicas. Excluded occurrences remain recorded outside the included-content denominator.

### Metrics, negative controls and gates

Report unsafe accepted cuts/accepted cuts; safe-cut coverage over **all human-SAFE candidates**, including missed proposals; UNKNOWN/UNSURE rates; content loss/duplication; justification residuals; per-slot deltas; measure-per-line distribution, including unknown counts; per-video results.

Inject cuts through **a tie, lyrics and a repeat sign**, including broken/blurred strokes and one-pixel dots. All must be rejected; add positive safe controls to expose an always-UNKNOWN classifier.

| Gate | Acceptance threshold |
|---|---|
| Safety | **0 human-UNSAFE accepted cuts**, 0 accepted UNSURE, 100% rejection of injected negatives; ≥100 human-verified accepted held-out cuts, otherwise insufficient evidence |
| Usefulness | ≥60% safe-cut coverage overall and ≥40% per held-out video; frozen denominators, no post-hoc removal of hard cases |
| Accounting | 0 lost, duplicated, clipped or occluded protected pixels; exact native reconstruction; 100% replica/scaffold operations logged |
| Layout | 0 lock violations/overflows; ≤8% expansion, ≤3% contraction; 100% slot-cap compliance; justified-edge residual ≤1 output px; every ragged line recorded |
| Persistence | 100% pass of stale-revision, conflicting-lock, undo/redo, restart and failed-commit preservation scenarios |

For zero errors in `n` accepted independent cuts, the one-sided 95% upper bound is `1−0.05^(1/n)`: approximately **2.95% at 100**, **1.49% at 200**. Correlated cuts and five videos weaken population claims; do not present this bound as a deployment guarantee.
